# TaraScan

A one-screen page scanner for iPhone that runs in the browser. Point the camera at a page, tap the shutter, repeat, then tap Save to get one PDF. There is no page limit.

**Open it:** https://senolgulgonul.github.io/tarascan/

**Version:** v4.0

## Use it

1. Open the link in Safari on your iPhone and allow camera access.
2. Tap the Share button and choose **Add to Home Screen** to get an app icon.
3. Hold the phone flat above the page and fill the frame with it. What you see on the screen is what goes into the PDF.
4. Tap the round button to take the photo. The photo is cleaned up: shadows are removed and the paper comes out white, also under warm room light. The last page appears bottom left.
5. Repeat for every page, then tap **Save (n)**. All pages are saved as one PDF named `Scan_date_time.pdf`, and a new scan starts.

Other controls:

- **Cancel** discards the pages scanned so far.
- Tapping the small picture of the last page removes that page, in case a shot went wrong.

Tips:

- TaraScan doesn’t crop or straighten the photo, so nothing on the page can be cut off by mistake. Frame the page the way you want it in the PDF.
- Avoid glare, and keep the phone parallel to the page so the text isn’t slanted.
- iPhone doesn’t let web apps fill in the email subject. TaraScan copies the file name when you tap Save, so you can paste it into the subject line.
- TaraScan also opens on a computer, but most webcams are low resolution and fixed focus, so small text comes out blurry. Use a phone for documents.

## Privacy

Photos and PDFs are processed on the phone. Nothing is uploaded. Pages in progress are kept in the browser’s storage on that device until you save or cancel.
